Corrective Action: CA-214
Related Finding: F-214
Date: 2026-02-20

The organization will extend the Supplier Verification Register so that all direct
and subcontracted suppliers whose material enters certified production are subject
to the same verification process.

All subcontracted material must be linked to an approved supplier entry before
being accepted into certified production.

The revised procedure will be communicated to procurement and receiving personnel.
