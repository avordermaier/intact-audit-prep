Title: Supplier Approval and Verification Procedure
Version: 4
Effective Date: 2026-08-01

Section 4 - Supplier Approval

All direct suppliers must be entered into the Supplier Verification Register
before material may be accepted into certified production.

Supplier approval must be reviewed annually by the Procurement Manager.

Section 5 - Receiving Controls

Receiving personnel must verify that incoming material originates from a supplier
listed in the Supplier Verification Register.
